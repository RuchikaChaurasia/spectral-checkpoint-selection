### Ablation 2: trigger patience

We varied how many consecutive epochs the mean alpha must stay below 2.0 before Rule W fires (1, 2 or 3 epochs; 2 is the main setting), keeping every other choice fixed. The three variants gave identical results: Rule W never fired in any of the 12 fine-tuning runs, so every variant fell back to the final epoch (30), and Real World macro-F1 was the same across patiences at every budget (10%: 0.354 ± 0.018, 25%: 0.468 ± 0.009, 50%: 0.497 ± 0.030, 100%: 0.519 ± 0.015; mean ± SD over three seeds). The reason is that the mean alpha over all layers stayed between 3.33 and 4.30 after epoch 1 and never approached the 2.0 threshold. Patience therefore cannot matter in this setting, and this ablation does not tell us how sensitive Rule W would be to patience in a setting where it fires. The finding is that a threshold of 2.0, taken from prior work, was not reached when fine-tuning an ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-18 on Office-Home for 30 epochs.
